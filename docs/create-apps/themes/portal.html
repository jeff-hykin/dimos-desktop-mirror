Motion, sound, icons

Portal's flourishes are the shell's: the chat box's pastel rim turns while it's in use and a smoky glow drifts around it; the agent-finished strip glitches in with a pink/blue split. Everything stops under reduced motion. An app moves with .15s ease on hover and focus, and plays no UI sounds of its own (the shell's synthesized packs do that).
